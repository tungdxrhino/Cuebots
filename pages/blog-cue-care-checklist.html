THE MATCH-READY CUE CARE CHECKLIST

A short routine after play protects the shaft, tip, joint and case without turning maintenance into a project.

CUEBOTS Equipment Team · Updated July 2026

ChecklistAfter every sessionWeekly checkStorageTravel day

After every session

  1. Wipe chalk and hand residue from the shaft with a clean, dry microfiber cloth.
  2. Inspect the tip edge for mushrooming or glazing.
  3. Clean visible joint surfaces without forcing debris into the threads.
  4. Return every piece to its fitted compartment.

Weekly inspection

Check that the bumper, joint collar and tip remain secure. Roll the assembled cue gently on a known-flat table only as a quick visual check; do not press down on the shaft.

Use material-appropriate productsA cleaner designed for carbon may not be suitable for unfinished wood, leather tips or decorative finishes. Follow the product maker's care instructions.

Store straight and climate-stable

Keep the cue in a structured case away from direct sunlight, heaters, vehicle interiors and damp areas. Avoid leaning an assembled cue against a wall for long periods.

Tournament travel day

  • Confirm every joint protector is fitted.
  • Pack chalk in a separate pouch.
  • Carry a clean cloth and tip tool.
  • Check that the case closes without pressure on the cue.
